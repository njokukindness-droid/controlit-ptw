How CONTROLIT Works

A complete guide to raising, approving, and closing digital Permits to Work — from first login to final sign-off.

User Roles
👤
HSE Manager Admin
Sets up the organisation. Approves permits, manages users, and has full visibility of all active permits on site.
📋
Permit Issuer Issuer
The authorised HSE officer or engineer who raises and submits permit requests on behalf of the team.
🪖
Permit Receiver Receiver
The worker or supervisor who accepts the permit, confirms the work is complete, and requests closure.
Permit Lifecycle

Status flow

Draft → Submitted → Approved → Active → Closed

Each permit moves through these stages. Work cannot legally begin until the permit is Active. A permit number is auto-generated the moment a permit is submitted — no WhatsApp required.

Getting Started — First-time Setup
1
Receive your login credentials
Your HSE Manager or organisation administrator will send you an email with your CONTROLIT login email and a temporary password. Keep these safe.
2
Log in at getcontrolit.com
Open getcontrolit.com in any browser — Chrome, Safari, or Edge on your phone or laptop. Enter your email and password. You will land directly on your organisation's dashboard.
💡 Add getcontrolit.com to your phone's home screen for quick access — tap Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone, or the browser menu on Android.
3
Change your password (recommended)
Go to Settings → Change Password and set a personal password. Use something you will remember — minimum 8 characters.
Raising a Permit Issuer
1
Tap "New Permit"
From the dashboard, tap the green + New Permit button. Select the permit type: Work at Height, Hot Work, Confined Space, Electrical Isolation, Lifting Operations, or Excavation.
2
Fill in the permit details
Complete all required fields: work description, location, start date and time, expected end date and time, risk level, and the name of the permit receiver (the worker on site).
⚠️ Be specific with the location — write the grid reference, floor level, or area name so the approver can verify the work without coming to site.
3
Select hazards and control measures
Tick all relevant hazards (working at height, hot surfaces, electrical, etc.) and confirm the control measures are in place. This becomes the risk record for the permit.
4
Submit the permit
Tap Submit for Approval. A unique permit number is generated instantly and the permit moves to Submitted. The HSE Manager is notified.
✅ No more posting screenshots in WhatsApp groups. Your permit number appears on screen immediately.
Approving a Permit HSE Manager
1
Review pending permits
On your dashboard, the Pending tab shows all permits waiting for your approval. Each card shows the permit type, location, issuer name, and risk level at a glance.
2
Open and review the permit
Tap a permit card to view the full details — work description, hazards, control measures, and receiver. Scroll through carefully before making a decision.
3
Approve or reject
Tap Approve to authorise the work. The permit moves to Approved. If there is an issue, tap Reject and add a comment explaining what needs to change.
⚠️ Approving a permit is a legal authorisation. Only approve when you are satisfied the controls are adequate and the receiver is competent.
Accepting & Activating Work Receiver
1
Receive the approved permit
Once the HSE Manager approves, the permit receiver (the worker on site) can see the permit in their My Permits tab with status Approved.
2
Confirm receipt and activate
Open the permit, read the conditions, then tap Accept & Activate. The permit moves to Active. Work can now begin.
✅ Both the issuer and the HSE Manager can see the permit is now Active and work is in progress.
Closing a Permit
1
Work is complete — request closure
When the work is finished, the receiver taps Request Closure on the active permit. Add any completion notes (what was done, any issues encountered).
2
HSE Manager confirms closure
The HSE Manager reviews the closure request and taps Close Permit. The permit status changes to Closed and is archived with a full audit trail.
3
Permit is archived automatically
All closed permits remain searchable in the History tab with dates, signatories, and all details intact — ready for audits or incident investigations at any time.
✅ Full digital audit trail: who raised it, who approved it, who accepted it, when work started, and when it was closed.
Important Rules & Reminders

⚠️ Never start work without an Active permit

A permit that is Submitted or Approved does NOT authorise work to begin. The receiver must Accept & Activate the permit first. Work before activation is an unauthorised activity.

🕐 Permits expire at the end date

If work is not complete before the permit end date, the permit expires and a new one must be raised. Never continue work on an expired permit.

🔒 One permit per activity

Each distinct work activity in each location requires its own permit. If you are doing hot work in two different locations, raise two separate permits.

📱 Works on any device

CONTROLIT works on any smartphone, tablet, or computer with a browser. No app download needed. Add getcontrolit.com to your home screen for the fastest access on site.
