How CONTROLIT Works

A complete guide to raising, approving, and closing digital Permits to Work — from first login to final sign-off.

User Roles
📋
Permit Issuer Issuer
Raises and submits the permit request. Usually the HSE Engineer or Permit Coordinator responsible for the work activity.
🏗️
Area Authority AA
The "landlord" of the work environment — typically the Project Manager. Gives permission for the activity to happen in their area and can identify SIMOP conflicts.
👤
HSE Manager HSE Mgr
Verifies HSE compliance after the Area Authority approves. Reviews control measures and can add additional controls before the HSE Officer proceeds.
🦺
HSE Officer Officer
Carries out the physical site check — walks the area, confirms controls are in place, and formally issues the permit.
🪖
Permit Receiver Receiver
The worker or supervisor on site who accepts the issued permit, confirms understanding of the conditions, and activates it to begin work.
Permit Lifecycle

Status flow

Draft → Submitted → AA Approved → HSE Verified → Issued → Active → Closed

Each permit passes through every stage in order. Work cannot legally begin until the permit is Active. A unique permit number is generated the moment a permit is submitted — no WhatsApp screenshots needed.

Getting Started — First-time Setup
1
Receive your login credentials
Your HSE Manager or organisation administrator will send you an email with your CONTROLIT login email and a temporary password. Keep these safe.
2
Log in at getcontrolit.com
Open getcontrolit.com in any browser — Chrome, Safari, or Edge on your phone or laptop. Enter your email and password. You will land directly on your organisation's dashboard.
💡 Add getcontrolit.com to your phone's home screen for quick access — tap Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone, or the browser menu on Android.
3
Change your password (recommended)
Go to 🔑 Change Password in the menu and set a personal password. Use something you will remember — minimum 8 characters.
Step 1 — Raising a Permit Issuer
1
Tap "New Permit"
From the dashboard, tap the green + New Permit button. Select the permit type: Work at Height, Hot Work, Confined Space, Electrical Isolation, Lifting Operations, or Excavation.
2
Fill in the permit details
Complete all required fields: work description, location, start and end date/time, risk level, and the name of the permit receiver (the worker on site).
⚠️ Be specific with the location — write the grid reference, floor level, or area name so reviewers can verify without coming to site.
3
Select hazards and control measures
Tick all relevant hazards and confirm the control measures that will be in place. This forms the HSE risk record attached to the permit.
4
Submit the permit
Tap Submit for Approval. A unique permit number is generated instantly. The permit moves to Submitted and the Area Authority is notified.
✅ Your permit number appears on screen immediately — no need to post screenshots in WhatsApp groups and wait for a reply.
Step 2 — Area Authority Approval AA
1
Review the permit request
The Area Authority (typically the Project Manager) receives a notification. As the owner of the work environment, they review the activity — where it is happening, when, and who is doing it.
2
Check for SIMOP conflicts
CONTROLIT shows other active and approved permits in the same area. If another job is already running in the same location at the same time (a Simultaneous Operation — SIMOP), the Area Authority can decline and prevent a dangerous clash.
⚠️ A SIMOP can be fatal. If you see a conflict, decline the permit and ask the issuer to reschedule or coordinate with the other team first.
3
Approve or decline
Tap Approve to confirm that the activity may proceed in your area. Tap Decline and add a reason if there is a conflict or the work is not suitable at this time. The permit then moves to the HSE Manager for verification.
Step 3 — HSE Verification HSE Mgr
1
Review HSE compliance
The HSE Manager receives the Area-Authority-approved permit. They verify that the hazards are correctly identified, the control measures are adequate, and the right people are assigned.
2
Add extra controls if needed
If additional precautions are required — a gas test, PPE upgrade, isolation certificate — the HSE Manager can add these before passing the permit forward. These become mandatory conditions.
3
Verify and pass to HSE Officer
Once satisfied, the HSE Manager marks the permit as HSE Verified and it moves to the HSE Officer for the physical site check.
Step 4 — Site Check & Issuance HSE Officer
1
Go to the work location
The HSE Officer physically visits the site where the work will take place. They check that the area is safe, barriers are in place, PPE is available, and the conditions match what was described in the permit.
2
Confirm all controls are in place
Verify every control measure listed on the permit — including any additional ones added by the HSE Manager. Do not issue the permit if any control is missing.
⚠️ This is the last line of defence before work begins. If something is not right on site, hold the permit until it is corrected.
3
Issue the permit
Tap Issue Permit. The permit status changes to Issued. The receiver is notified that the permit is ready to be accepted.
Step 5 — Accepting & Activating Work Receiver
1
Receive the issued permit
The receiver sees the permit in their My Permits tab with status Issued. Open it and read all conditions carefully before accepting.
2
Accept and activate
Tap Accept & Activate. The permit moves to Active. Work can now begin. By accepting, you confirm that you have read and understood all permit conditions.
✅ All stakeholders — Issuer, Area Authority, HSE Manager, and Officer — can see the permit is Active and work is in progress.
Step 6 — Closing a Permit
1
Work is complete — request closure
When the work is finished, the receiver taps Request Closure on the active permit. Add any completion notes (what was done, any issues encountered).
2
HSE Officer / HSE Manager confirms closure
The HSE Officer or HSE Manager reviews the closure request and taps Close Permit. The permit status changes to Closed and is archived with a full audit trail.
3
Permit is archived automatically
All closed permits remain searchable in the History tab with dates, signatories, and all details intact — ready for audits or incident investigations at any time.
✅ Full digital audit trail: who raised it, who approved it, who accepted it, when work started, and when it was closed.
Important Rules & Reminders

⚠️ Never start work without an Active permit

A permit that is Submitted, AA Approved, HSE Verified, or even Issued does NOT yet authorise work to begin. The receiver must Accept & Activate the permit first. Starting work before activation is an unauthorised activity.

🕐 Permits expire at the end date

If work is not complete before the permit end date, the permit expires and a new one must be raised. Never continue work on an expired permit.

🔒 One permit per activity

Each distinct work activity in each location requires its own permit. If you are doing hot work in two different locations, raise two separate permits.

📱 Works on any device

CONTROLIT works on any smartphone, tablet, or computer with a browser. No app download needed. Add getcontrolit.com to your home screen for the fastest access on site.
