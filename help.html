How CONTROLIT Works

A complete guide to raising, approving, and closing digital Permits to Work — from first login to final sign-off.

The Four Login Roles
📝
Requester Requester
The person performing the job or supervising it. Fills in the permit form and submits the request. At the end of the job, they become the Receiver — accepting the issued permit and initiating closeout.
🏗️
Area Authority AA
The owner of the asset or work environment — typically the Project Manager. Gives permission for the activity to happen in their area and can identify dangerous SIMOP conflicts before approving.
👤
HSE Manager HSE Mgr
Senior issuing authority. Reviews and verifies HSE compliance after the Area Authority approves. Can add mandatory extra controls before the HSE Officer proceeds to site.
🦺
HSE Officer HSE Officer
The issuer. Receives the HSE-Manager-verified permit, goes to the physical work location, ticks off every control measure on site, and formally issues the permit. Also verifies the closeout at the end.

🔄 Requester becomes Receiver

The person who raises the permit request is the same person who receives and activates it once it is issued. They are called the Requester at the start and the Receiver at the end. The permit form also captures the Supervisor's name (construction manager, site engineer, or foreman) as a field — the supervisor does not need a separate login.

Permit Lifecycle

Status flow

Draft → Submitted → AA Approved → HSE Verified → Issued → Active → Closed

Each permit must pass through every stage in this exact order. Work cannot begin until the permit is Active. A unique permit number is generated the moment the permit is submitted — no WhatsApp screenshots needed.

Getting Started — First-time Setup
1
Receive your login credentials
Your HSE Manager or organisation administrator will send you an email with your CONTROLIT login email and a temporary password. Keep these safe.
2
Log in at getcontrolit.com
Open getcontrolit.com in any browser — Chrome, Safari, or Edge on your phone or laptop. Enter your email and password. You will land directly on your organisation's dashboard.
💡 Add getcontrolit.com to your phone's home screen for quick access — tap Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone, or the browser menu on Android.
3
Change your password (recommended)
Go to 🔑 Change Password in the menu and set a personal password. Use something you will remember — minimum 8 characters.
Step 1 — Submitting a Permit Request Requester
1
Tap "New Permit"
From the dashboard, tap the green + New Permit button. Select the permit type: Work at Height, Hot Work, Confined Space, Electrical Isolation, Lifting Operations, or Excavation.
2
Fill in the permit details
Complete all required fields: work description, location, start and end date/time, risk level, and your supervisor's name (construction manager, site engineer, or foreman). The supervisor's name is captured here as a field — they do not need a separate login.
💡 If the person performing the job cannot read or write, the supervisor can log in with their own credentials and fill in the form on their behalf — entering the job performer's name in the performer field and their own name in the supervisor field. The printed permit will show the declaration: "I am fully in charge of this job and have designated [name] as the person performing the work."
⚠️ Be specific with the location — write the grid reference, floor level, or area name so reviewers can understand the exact scope without coming to site first.
3
Select hazards and control measures
Tick all relevant hazards (working at height, hot surfaces, electrical hazards, etc.) and confirm the control measures that will be in place. Be thorough — the HSE Manager may add extra controls if they feel this section is incomplete.
4
Submit the request
Tap Submit. A unique permit number is generated instantly and the permit moves to Submitted. The Area Authority receives a notification to review the request.
✅ Your permit number appears on screen immediately — no need to post screenshots in a WhatsApp group and wait for someone to reply with a number.
Step 2 — Area Authority Approval AA
1
Log in and review the request
The Area Authority (typically the Project Manager) receives a notification and logs in to review. As the owner of the work environment — the asset, the zone, the floor — they check what activity is being requested, where, when, and by whom.
2
Check for SIMOP conflicts
CONTROLIT displays other active and approved permits in the same area. If another team is already working in the same location at the same time — a Simultaneous Operation (SIMOP) — the Area Authority can spot the clash and decline before it becomes dangerous.
⚠️ SIMOPs can be fatal — hot work next to a confined space entry, lifting over an active work area. If you see a conflict, decline and ask the teams to coordinate and resubmit at a safe time.
3
Approve or decline
Tap Approve to confirm the activity may proceed in your area. Tap Decline and add a reason if there is a conflict or the timing is not right. On approval, the HSE Manager is notified automatically.
Step 3 — HSE Verification HSE Mgr
1
Review the Area-Authority-approved permit
The HSE Manager receives the permit after the Area Authority approves. Their job is not to approve the job itself — the Area Authority already did that — but to verify that the HSE side is properly covered: hazards correctly identified, controls adequate, right people assigned.
2
Add extra controls if needed
If additional precautions are required — a gas test before entry, specific PPE, an isolation certificate, a toolbox talk — the HSE Manager can add these as mandatory conditions on the permit before it goes forward.
3
Approve and pass to HSE Officer
Once satisfied, the HSE Manager approves. The permit status moves to HSE Verified and the HSE Officer is notified to carry out the physical site check.
Step 4 — Physical Site Check & Issuance HSE Officer
1
Go to the work location
The HSE Officer physically goes to the site where the work will take place. Open CONTROLIT on your phone, pull up the permit, and use it as your checklist while you inspect the area.
2
Tick off every control measure
Verify every control listed on the permit — including any extras added by the HSE Manager. Check that barriers are erected, PPE is on site, gas tests have been done, isolations are in place, and the area matches the permit description.
⚠️ This is the final check before work begins. If any control is missing or the site conditions differ from the permit, do not issue. Hold the permit and notify the requester to correct it first.
3
Issue the permit
When everything is in order, tap Issue Permit. The status changes to Issued and the requester is notified that their permit is ready to be accepted.
Step 5 — Accepting & Starting Work Receiver
1
Receive and read the issued permit
The requester — now acting as the Receiver — sees the permit in their My Permits tab with status Issued. Open it and read all conditions and controls carefully before accepting.
2
Accept and activate
Tap Accept & Activate. The permit moves to Active. Work can now begin. By accepting, you confirm you have read and understood every condition on the permit.
✅ All parties — Area Authority, HSE Manager, and HSE Officer — can now see the permit is Active and work is in progress on their dashboards.
Step 6 — Closeout Receiver + HSE Officer
1
Receiver initiates closeout
When the work is finished and the area is made safe, the Receiver taps Request Closure on the active permit. Add completion notes — what was done, any issues encountered, whether the area has been restored.
2
HSE Officer verifies and closes
The HSE Officer reviews the closure request, confirms the work is complete and the area is safe, and taps Close Permit. The permit moves to Closed and is archived with the full audit trail.
3
Permit archived with full history
All closed permits remain permanently searchable in the History tab — every name, date, control measure, and sign-off recorded. Ready for audits, incident investigations, or client reviews at any time.
✅ Complete digital audit trail: who requested it, who the supervisor was, who the Area Authority was, what HSE controls were added, who issued it, who accepted it, and when it was closed.
Important Rules & Reminders

⚠️ Never start work without an Active permit

A permit that is Submitted, AA Approved, HSE Verified, or even Issued does NOT yet authorise work to begin. The receiver must Accept & Activate the permit first. Starting work before activation is an unauthorised activity.

🕐 Permits expire at the end date

If work is not complete before the permit end date, the permit expires and a new one must be raised. Never continue work on an expired permit.

🔒 One permit per activity

Each distinct work activity in each location requires its own permit. If you are doing hot work in two different locations, raise two separate permits.

📱 Works on any device

CONTROLIT works on any smartphone, tablet, or computer with a browser. No app download needed. Add getcontrolit.com to your home screen for the fastest access on site.
